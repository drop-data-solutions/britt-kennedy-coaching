Recipes

From Britt's kitchen

Two of Britt's own recipes, with short ingredient lists and simple steps. They go great on the same plate.

Meatloaf

Ingredients

  • 2 pounds ground beef
  • Half a cup of breadcrumbs
  • 1 egg
  • Salt, pepper, garlic and dried onion
  • A couple tablespoons of soy sauce

Steps

  1. Mix it all up.
  2. Bake it in a pan at 350°F for 45–50 minutes, until the center reaches 160°F.

Whipped sweet potatoes

Ingredients

  • 6 sweet potatoes
  • 2 or 3 tablespoons of butter
  • A splash of milk
  • A little garlic powder

Steps

  1. Boil the sweet potatoes until soft, then drain.
  2. Whip everything together with a hand mixer.

FAQ

Good questions, straight answers

Do I need a Big North Barbell membership?

Not for nutrition coaching, though gym members pay a little less for it. For personal training, non-members pay Big North Barbell a $25/month gym fee.

Is a 30-minute session enough?

It depends on your goals. Personal training comes in 30- and 60-minute sessions, and nutrition sessions are 30 minutes. Britt can help you choose the length that fits your goals and schedule.

What does a nutrition coach do?

A nutrition coach helps you build eating and lifestyle habits that fit your goals. Britt holds an NCI Level 1 Nutrition certification and offers single 30-minute sessions or ongoing monthly coaching with personalized macro goals and bi-weekly check-ins.

Can I work with Britt if I don't live nearby?

Yes. You can do nutrition coaching from anywhere in the country, with check-ins on Zoom. Personal training is in person at Big North Barbell, and Britt also writes training plans for people outside the area or training at home.

Do you offer couples and besties training?

Yes. Two people training together pay $70 for 30 minutes or $120 for 60 minutes. Book a free consult to get started together.

Can I combine personal training and nutrition coaching?

Yes. Personal training clients can add monthly nutrition coaching for $50/month. Book a free consult to set it up.
